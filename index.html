Senku Ishigami

Welcome to my first web page. It is about Senku, the main character of the anime Dr. Stone.

About Senku

Senku Ishigami is a genius high school student who loves science. His famous words are "This is exhilarating!" when he gets excited about a discovery.

One day, a mysterious light turns all humans into stone. Senku wakes up about 3,700 years later and decides to rebuild civilization using science.

His Friends

  • Taiju - strong and loyal best friend
  • Yuzuriha - kind and skilled with her hands
  • Chrome - a curious villager who becomes his assistant

Senku's Plan to Rebuild the World

  1. Wake up in the Stone World after 3,700 years
  2. Revive Taiju to get help
  3. Make useful things with science, like soap, medicine and glass
  4. Build the Kingdom of Science with new friends
  5. Bring back modern technology step by step
